V:TES Crypt Draw Simulator

Start with at least N, at most N, exactly N, no or any, then where and filters on cap, disc, name, title and clan.

Query syntax

A query describes your opening hand. It is made of one or more clauses: each counts the vampires in the hand that match some filters. 

<quantifier> [distinct] [vampire(s)] where <filters>

Quantifiers

at least NN or more matches
at most NN or fewer matches
exactly Nexactly N matches
anyat least one match
nozero matches

Without distinct every copy counts: two copies of the same vampire are 2 matches. With distinct they count once.

Filters

FilterOperatorsValues
cap / capacity = != < <= > >= a whole number
disc / discipline = != in POT (uppercase) requires superior; pot (lowercase) accepts either level
name = != ~ in "Anson"
title = != ~ in prince, baron, inner circle, … plus any (titled) and none (untitled)
clan = != ~ in Ventrue, Daughter of Cacophony

~ means "contains", e.g. name ~ arika matches both base and advanced versions. in (a, b) matches any of the listed values. Matching ignores case (except the superior/inferior rule for disciplines). Quote values that contain commas, parentheses or the words and/or.

Combining

Use and, or, not and parentheses, both between filters and between whole clauses. and binds tighter than or.

Examples

Click an example to use it.

  • any where title = any at least one titled vampire
  • no vampire where title = any no titled vampires at all
  • at least 2 distinct where cap <= 4 two different small vampires
  • at least 1 where disc = DOM and (title in (prince, justicar) or cap > 9) superior Dominate on a prince, justicar or big vampire
  • any where name = "Anson" or any where name = "Lodin" either of two specific vampires
  • at least 1 where clan = Brujah and no vampire where cap >= 10 a Brujah, and nothing at capacity 10 or more
  • exactly 1 where name ~ arika exactly one copy of any Arika
